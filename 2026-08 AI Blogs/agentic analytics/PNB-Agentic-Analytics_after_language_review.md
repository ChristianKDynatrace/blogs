From alert to remediation: smarter agentic investigations in Dynatrace

**When something breaks in a complex production environment, you need to
get from \"something\'s wrong\" to \"here\'s the problem," and "here\'s
the fix\" as quickly as possible. The most recent updates to Dynatrace
Intelligence and Dynatrace Assist make that jump shorter and faster,
with sharper reasoning and deeper data intelligence . Whether you\'re
chatting with Assist, running an autonomous, agentic workflow, or
connecting an external agent via the Model Context Protocol (MCP), the
same upgraded foundation underpins them all.**

## Investigations, reimagined from the ground up

Production incidents can propagate at machine speed. A latency spike
might trace two hops upstream to a service you didn\'t initially
suspect. A log anomaly might only make sense in the context of a recent
deployment and a shift in a downstream dependency. Following that trail,
correlating signals, keeping the thread coherent, and knowing when the
evidence is strong enough to act are what separate a fast resolution
from a long one.

### Dynatrace has the foundation for this: 

-   [**Grail**](http://www.dynatrace.com/platform/grail), our unified
    data lakehouse, manages every signal type in your environment.

**Smartscape**, the real-time dependency graph, shows all your
environment\'s dependencies, end to end for more detailed and precise
insights.

[This update brings](http://www.dynatrace.com/platform/grail) a
significantly improved reasoning engine that can follow your dependency
graph where the evidence leads, with proprietary Dynatrace expertise
encoded into every investigation, and an interface that keeps the thread
alive as you work.

### Analytics that understand your data, not just your question

Asking a question in natural language and getting back a reliable answer
requires more than a capable reasoning model. It requires deep fluency
in how your data is actually structured: what your metrics mean, how
your logs are shaped, and what a meaningful query looks like for a given
signal type.

Behind that experience is a purpose-built Dynatrace AI model, trained on
hundreds of thousands of real DQL queries. It has internalized the
structure of Dynatrace data the way a fluent practitioner would, not by
looking up examples, but by understanding the underlying patterns.

That capability runs through Dynatrace Assist,
[Notebooks](https://www.dynatrace.com/hub/detail/notebooks/),
[Dashboards](https://www.dynatrace.com/hub/detail/dashboards), and the
[Dynatrace MCP
server](https://docs.dynatrace.com/docs/dynatrace-intelligence/dynatrace-mcp).
Ask a data question anywhere in Dynatrace, in natural language, and the
intelligence behind it is the same.

## The investigation playbook, encoded

This update improves a curated set of Dynatrace investigation skills
that reflect how experienced practitioners actually approach problems,
so every investigation starts from the right entry point without you
having to specify it. The skills this reasoning engine leverages include
advanced root cause analysis, telemetry correlation, and
hyperscaler-specific observability across AWS, Azure, and Kubernetes,
among others.

This matters equally whether you\'re working interactively in Assist or
for autonomous agents, whether they run inside Dynatrace or connect via
MCP. In both cases, the investigation opens with the same structured
expertise a senior SRE would apply, rather than reasoning from scratch
each time as many other products on the market do.

Later this year, you\'ll be able to contribute your own skills alongside
ours. Your team\'s investigation patterns and the institutional
knowledge of how your specific environment behaves will run through the
same engine. The expertise stops being \"what Dynatrace knows\" and
becomes \"what your team knows,\" compounding over time.

## Try it on your next incident

Turn on agentic mode and put it to work on a real problem. Just ask
Assist what\'s wrong: it will reason through the problem for you, call
Dynatrace tools, apply curated expertise, and follow the dependency
graph upstream where the evidence leads. All the while, you stay in
control; Assist does the analytical heavy lifting, correlates the
signals, traces the root cause, and proposes a remediation for your
approval before applying it autonomously.
