## Five use cases groups
- Incident Analysis: our core strength – root cause analysis, anomaly detection, causality, surface related information, recommendation …
- Optimize & Tune: a unique value competitors can’t claim – using our forecasting for performance optimization, cost optimization …
- Agentic Reporting: surface insights - combine agentic analysis and compare status quo with the past to identify patterns, show trends, analyze changes …
- Risk & Impact: what if, from past to future – don’t stop at analyzing what has happened, but simulate what might happen and why I should care
- Security: way more than infra, apps and services – use agentic AI to improve resilience, validate findings, correlate vulnerabilities with problems, …
